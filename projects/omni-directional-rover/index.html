Omni Directional Rover

2026

This is part one of what will hopefully become a more advanced project with computer vision, autonomous movement, a robotic arm, and more. For now, this is a three-wheeled rover with omnidirectional wheels, which lets it move in any direction. It uses geared DC motors with encoders and is controlled over Wi-Fi with an ESP32.
