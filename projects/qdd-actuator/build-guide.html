QDD Actuator Build Guide

Here's the build guide for my internal QDD actuator. The project write-up is on the QDD Internal Actuator page.

Materials

ItemQuantity
45x55x6 bearing3
15x21x5 bearing1
6x12x4 bearing7
12x5x3 magnets42
3x30 metal dowel3
M3 25mm bolt3
8110 stator1
26 gauge copper wire1
M3 heated inserts34
MKS ODrive mini + wire + magnet1
Silicon grease
General M3 bolts

1. Wind the stator

Wind the stator with 26 gauge wire according to the diagram. I was able to get 24 turns per tooth. More turns would be better if you can. You can also get multiple spools of wire and use multiple wires at once. I don't have a picture for it but the ends should be connected and covered with heat shrink, and the start of each wire should also be connected to a protected wire and covered with heat shrink. I used the wires that came with the motor driver, but anything that plugs into the motor driver is fine.

2. Inner output

Press in the 15x21x5 bearing into the inner output and insert 3 dowel pins.

3. Housing and stator

Get the main housing ready and insert the stator.

After press fighting the bearing into the housing, the stator will not fit. The stator must be put in first.

4. First large bearing

Press fit a 45x55x7 bearing into the inside and push in the inner output.

5. Planet gears

Fit 6x12x5 bearings into both sides of the planet gears.

6. Install the planets

Insert the planet gears into the housing. It should not require much force, just wiggle them around so they go in.

Make sure the gears spin smoothly before going forward.

7. Grease and outer bearing

Liberally grease with silicone grease the gears and press in the outer 45x55x7 bearing.

8. Outer output

With the outer output, insert a 6x12x5 bearing and add the heat set inserts to the holes that do not go all the way through.

9. Close the gearbox

Push the outer output into the 45x55x7 bearing by lining up the holes with the dowel pins. On the other side, screw in 25mm bolts to keep the gearbox together. Don't screw in too tight as it could cause the gearbox to spin off centre. Now is also when I put in the heat set inserts for screwing in the backplate.

10. Outside inserts

Now is when I added the heat set inserts on the outside, but this could have been done earlier.

11. Rotor magnets

Now to finish the rotor. I used 42 n52 magnets and superglue. The magnets need to be glued in alternating polarity, so make sure to test each one as you go.

12. Encoder magnet

Now press the last 45x55x7 bearing to the back of the rotor and glue on the encoder magnet. Be very careful that the encoder magnet is flat.

13. Insert the rotor

Now the rotor can be inserted in the gearbox. The magnets will want to stick to the stator, but if you press hard enough and centered enough it shouldn't be too hard. The bearings will help guide the rotor into the right position.

14. Back plate

Push the back plate onto the back of the rotor, the surface should be flat with the bearing. Before putting the back plate on, I added 4 heated inserts for the motor driver.

15. Motor driver

Finally, the motor driver can be screwed in. It came with a metal plate, which was removed, but I kept the little white plastic spacers. Make sure the wires align with the inputs on the motor driver.

16. Finished

And it's finished!
